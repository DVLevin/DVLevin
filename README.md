# Dima Levin

Dima Levin is the Chief AI Officer and co-founder of Cone Red, a Barcelona-based B2B AI automation lab whose 11 data scientists ship enterprise AI for n5bank (fintech licensing & M&A platform), VESCO (€133M industrial mining operation), and anri-pharm (Ukrainian pharmacy chain, 100+ locations), among others. Across 12+ years he has personally led AI systems for HSBC, Bank of America, Hyundai, Raiffeisen Bank International, ArcelorMittal, and Metinvest, in prior roles at Silent Eight (AML), Raiffeisen Bank International (Vienna AI Operations), and Intech Group (which acquired his AI/IoT consultancy in 2020). He is also CEO and co-founder of GetDeal.AI, an AI-native M&A marketplace for venture capital and AI-company acquisitions, and of BankStore, an AI platform connecting startups to 70+ banks and 180+ payment providers. PhD candidate in Smart Cities & AI; portfolio spans Ukraine, Finland, Spain, Belgium, and Austria.

🌿 **Currently:** running production multi-agent systems at Cone Red, writing the *In Easy Words* series on dimalevin.com, and finishing a paper on anticipation in artificial life.
